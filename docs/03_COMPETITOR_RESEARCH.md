Snapshot: 19 September 2026. **The broad product idea already exists.** Several products claim AI intake, dispatch and follow-through. No hands-on vendor evaluation was performed.

## Product findings and evidence

**Fixflo / Aidenn — very close UK incumbent.** Fixflo provides reporting, audit communications, workflows and a contractor marketplace. Aidenn claims diagnostic intake, photo interpretation, onward-work reports and emergency dispatch. These claims directly invalidate a pitch that incumbent systems merely log tickets. The reviewed AI page contains inconsistent training-volume language; do not repeat it as a verified statistic. [Fixflo](https://www.fixflo.com/), [Aidenn](https://www.fixflo.com/solutions/aidenn-ai-repairs-maintenance), [workflow](https://www.fixflo.com/features/workflow-management).

**Plentific — serious operating-platform competitor and possible future integration.** Accessible official navigation lists field service, contractor management, resident experience, AI, projects and work orders. The AI/work-order pages returned almost no extractable body text. Therefore detailed autonomy, voice and dependency claims remain U, rather than being filled in from assumptions. [Official product navigation](https://www.plentific.com/careers/), [AI product](https://www.plentific.com/ai-powered-property-operations/), [work orders](https://www.plentific.com/work-order-management/).

**Property Meld / MAX / Mezo — very close coordination and voice competitor.** Mezo was acquired in January 2025; treating it as an independent current startup would be misleading. MAX advertises scheduling and follow-up automation; On-Call handles repair intake and status calls. Project coordination is advertised. Its exact ability to infer and recover a new prerequisite needs a product test. Do not adopt its accuracy or savings claims as independently verified. [Acquisition/current product](https://propertymeld.com/property-meld-mezo/), [MAX](https://propertymeld.com/max-intelligence/), [feature/pricing detail](https://propertymeld.com/pricing/).

**Arthur — workflow incumbent.** Tenant and contractor interfaces sit alongside configurable workflow automation. The reviewed evidence supports operational automation, not a claim of an autonomous LLM repair coordinator. [Arthur](https://www.arthuronline.co.uk/), [workflow product](https://www.arthuronline.co.uk/features/workflow-management).

**MRI — suite, not one feature.** Housing, asset/repairs and customer-experience modules overlap the full operating process. Feature availability depends on product and integration. Do not score the whole suite as lacking AI or scheduling because one page omits it. [Housing](https://www.mrisoftware.com/uk/solutions/social-housing/), [asset/repairs](https://www.mrisoftware.com/uk/solutions/social-housing/asset-and-repairs/).

**Re-Leased — adjacent commercial-property competitor.** Maintenance includes contractor quotes, assignment, approvals, reminders and records. Credia Action creates work from emails with approval. Commercial lease obligations make its target operating model different from a residential repair desk. [Operations](https://www.re-leased.com/product/property-operations-maintenance), [Credia overview](https://www.re-leased.com/).

**Buildium — stronger overlap than a conventional ticketing label suggests.** Its current page advertises Lumina AI Maintenance Agent summarization, vendor/task progress monitoring and closing the loop, plus projects and a maintenance contact centre. Follow-up autonomy must therefore be treated as an existing competitor claim. [Maintenance product](https://www.buildium.com/features/property-management-maintenance-software/).

**AppFolio — explicit autonomous-coordinator competitor.** Maintenance Performer responds to residents, interprets images, triages emergencies and dispatches technicians, including a Lula vendor-network path. Scheduling also uses platform tools. Its broad positioning is extremely close; the evidence does not prove arbitrary dependency replanning. [Maintenance Performer](https://www.appfolio.com/property-manager/maintenance), [Realm-X](https://www.appfolio.com/ai).

**Propertyware — US single-family operating system.** Work orders, vendor management, contact-centre intake and open API create material overlap, but the reviewed material did not substantiate autonomous dependency reasoning. [Platform](https://www.propertyware.com/), [work-order product](https://www.propertyware.com/work-order-tracking-app/).

**askporter — closest UK follow-through positioning.** It explicitly describes a journey spanning triage, approved work, scheduling, access confirmation, updates and feedback; its example automatically reschedules when a resident cannot attend. This disproves the broad claim that competitors stop after intake. [Platform](https://www.askporter.com/), [repair journey](https://www.askporter.com/housing/repair-journey).

**EliseAI — strong voice/resident-workflow competitor.** Maintenance includes voice requests, work-order creation/categorization, technician routing, scheduling and proactive updates. A maintenance app supports delivery. Dynamic cross-trade prerequisite inference remains unverified. [Maintenance](https://eliseai.com/maintenance).

**Latchel — service plus software alternative.** Advertises an AI maintenance engine, dispatch and 24/7 call handling with humans available. The practical buyer alternative may be outsourced coordination rather than another software licence. [Latchel](https://latchel.com/).

## Can we claim uniqueness?

No. Public evidence establishes substantial overlap. It neither proves nor disproves that a competitor can execute the exact roof–scaffold–roof loop. Most marketing pages do not document internal state semantics or exception recovery.

Use this wording: **“Our prototype makes dependency-aware repair recovery inspectable: evidence in, policy-checked action out, then durable continuation.”**

Do not use: “First AI maintenance coordinator,” “competitors are only chatbots,” or “nobody handles follow-on work.”

## Comparative test to run after the event

Ask each shortlisted vendor to demonstrate the same sequence: failed attendance report → newly created prerequisite → approval → completion evidence → original order resumed → resident disputes completion. Check duplicate webhooks, cancellation, contradictory reports, operator override and restart recovery. Request actual audit records and API contracts.

A feature U becomes Y only with evidence. Marketing claims remain claims until tested. No vendor was contacted during this task.
